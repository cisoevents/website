## 2. MISSION, VALUES & PHILOSOPHY

**Mission Statement:**  
CISOevents exists to create a trusted space where security leaders can speak candidly, learn from peers, and shape the future of the industry — without vendor pressure or marketing noise.

**Philosophy:**  
*"The best security conversations happen when the room is full of practitioners who've actually done the work — not people trying to sell something."*

### Core Values

1. **Peer-Led Conversations**  
   Sessions are candid and off-record by default, creating a safe space for honest discussion. No sales pitches, no product demos.

2. **Vetted Community**  
   Attendance is curated. We review attendees to ensure the right people are in the room — qualified security professionals who add value to conversation.

3. **Practitioner Speakers Only**  
   All speakers are active practitioners — CISOs, Deputy CISOs, VPs of Security, or equivalent. We do not allow vendor marketers or sales representatives to present.

4. **National Reach**  
   Events span San Francisco, New York, Chicago, Toronto, London, and regional cities — building a truly national and international network.

5. **Vendor-Neutral**  
   CISOevents does not endorse products, services, or sponsors. Our platform is designed to benefit practitioners first.

6. **Operated by Neptune Media LLC**  
   CISOevents is a Neptune Media company. Neptune Media operates across 30+ media markets.

## 3. EVENT FORMATS

CISOevents runs three primary event formats:

### Horizon Summit (Flagship Annual Event)
- **Scale:** 150–250 CISOs and security executives
- **Duration:** Full day (sometimes two days)
- **Format:** Keynotes, expert panels, roundtable discussions, hands-on workshops, networking dinner
- **Purpose:** The flagship annual gathering for the cybersecurity community — big ideas, deep conversations, strategic networking
- **Typical ticket types:** General Admission, VIP (includes lunch and dinner)

### Roundtables (Invite-Only Executive Dinners)
- **Scale:** 15–25 senior security leaders
- **Format:** Small-group discussion over lunch or dinner; no slides, no demos, no vendor presentations
- **Atmosphere:** Off-the-record by default; focused on candid peer exchange
- **Selection:** Attendance by invitation only; curated by CISOevents team

### Virtual Briefings
- **Format:** Short-form online sessions covering emerging threats, regulatory shifts, or breaking cybersecurity news
- **Audience:** Open to broader security community
- **Duration:** Typically 60–90 minutes
- **Access:** Virtual attendance via link provided upon registration
